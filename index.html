Stanford HAI & Khatib Lab Operational Space Methodologies

Human-Centered Alignment for Safe Robotic Manipulation

Ph.D Scholar Lead Focus • Samad Saifi

As robotic systems step out of industrial safety cages and enter hospitals, schools, and homes, a fundamental paradigm shift is required. Safety cannot be treated as a simple software rule—it must be baked into the physics, dynamics, and real-time path planning of the system.

Download Full PDF download

Paper Abstract

This paper explores the physical and cognitive integration between humans and robotic manipulators. Drawing from Stanford's Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI) principles and Oussama Khatib's landmark Operational Space Formulation, we outline a compliant control matrix. By incorporating compliance over rigidity, implementing lightweight macro-mini actuation structures, and executing dynamic elastic strip path deforming algorithms, we build physical safety into the lowest mathematical loops of robotic systems.
